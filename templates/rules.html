HOW TO PLAY CRICKET CLASH

Based on the traditional Indian Hand Cricket / Odd or Even game, upgraded into a competitive modern web game.

🪙

1. The Odd-Even Toss

At the start of every match:

  • Choose your call: 1. Odd or 2. Even.
  • Enter any number between 0 and 6.
  • The computer simultaneously picks a number between 0 and 6.
  • Sum = Your Number + Computer Number.
  • If the sum parity matches your call, you win the toss and choose to Bat or Bowl!
🏏

2. Batting & Scoring Runs

When you are batting:

  • Select a shot number from 0 to 6.
  • If your number matches the bowler's number → OUT! (Wicket lost).
  • If the numbers are different → You score runs equal to your chosen number!
  • 0 = Dot ball (Safe defense, zero runs).
  • 4 = Boundary Four! • 6 = Maximum Six!
🎯

3. Bowling & Taking Wickets

When you are bowling:

  • Select your delivery number from 0 to 6.
  • Anticipate what the computer batsman will play.
  • If your delivery matches the computer's number → WICKET! The computer is OUT!
  • If they differ, computer adds their chosen number to their total score.
🏁

4. Chasing & Winning

Match conclusion rules:

  • Target: In the 2nd innings, the chasing side needs 1 run more than the 1st innings total (`Target = 1st Innings Runs + 1`).
  • Instant Win: As soon as the chasing team exceeds the 1st innings score, they win immediately!
  • All Out / Overs: If all wickets fall or overs finish before target is reached, the defending side wins!
  • Tie / Draw: If scores are exactly equal when the 2nd innings ends, the match is drawn.
