How Hand Cricket Works

In Hand Cricket (Odd-Even), both the batsman and bowler select a number from 0 to 6 simultaneously.

  • 💥 Same Number? OUT! The batsman loses a wicket.
  • 🏏 Different Numbers? Batsman scores runs equal to their chosen number!
  • 🪙 Toss: Call Odd or Even, throw numbers, and choose Bat or Bowl.
